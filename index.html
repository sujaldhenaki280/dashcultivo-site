Why speed matters
Companies that responded to a new lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as those that waited even one hour longer.
Harvard Business Review, "The Short Life of Online Sales Leads" (2011). Read the study

Couples and renters email a few places at once. When you're running an event or done for the day, their message waits, and the first good reply usually gets the tour.
